Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ArrowRove - Route Quest (referred to as “the Game” or “the Service”) collects, uses, stores and shares information when you install, access or play it. By downloading, launching or continuing to use the Service, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy and agree to the practices described below. If you do not accept these terms, please uninstall and stop using the Service.

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by a new “Last Updated” date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this page regularly so you stay informed about how your data is handled.

1. What Information We Process

We keep our data collection limited to what is needed to run the game, keep it stable, and improve your experience. Depending on how you use the Service, the following categories of information may be involved:

Account and Profile Information

If you sign in through a supported third party account such as Apple ID or Google, we receive the basic details that provider shares with us, including your email address, display name and profile picture. We use this information only to create your profile, confirm your identity, and keep your progress synchronized across the devices you use.

Device and Technical Information

We automatically collect technical details about the device you play on, including the advertising identifier assigned to your device (IDFA on iOS and GAID on Android), your public IP address, the device model, the operating system version, screen resolution, system language, time zone, and the type of network you are connected to (Wi Fi or mobile data). This helps us adapt the game to different hardware, diagnose connection problems, and protect the Service from suspicious activity.

Gameplay and Usage Records

We record anonymous information about how you play, such as the route levels you attempt, your completion times, how often you restart a stage, which tools or boosts you use, how long each play session lasts, and how you interact with in game rewards and advertisements. We study these records to balance the difficulty of route quests, build new stages, and refine the overall design of the Service.

Diagnostic and Error Logs

When the Game freezes, crashes or behaves abnormally, our systems may automatically generate logs that capture error codes and information about the running environment at that moment. These logs are used only to find and fix technical faults and to improve stability. They do not identify you personally.

Installed Application Information (Optional)

On first launch, an advertising partner integrated into the Game may ask for permission to view the list of applications installed on your device. You are free to allow or deny this request, and your choice does not affect your ability to use the core gameplay. The developer of ArrowRove - Route Quest does not see or store this list. It is managed solely by the advertising provider under its own terms.

2. How We Use the Information

All information we collect is used only for the lawful purposes described here, and we never apply it to unrelated activities without your separate consent:

  • To operate the Game and synchronize your route progress, completed stages and earned rewards across your accounts and devices.
  • To improve the interface, loading speed and compatibility of the Service across a wide range of mobile devices.
  • To serve appropriate advertisements and optional reward based tasks with the help of advertising identifiers.
  • To receive, review and respond to your feedback, bug reports and support requests.
  • To send in app notices about new route quests, updates to the Game, service changes and policy revisions.
  • To detect unusual accounts, reward abuse and other behaviour that could harm the normal operation of the Service.

3. When We Share Information

We treat your privacy seriously and do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone for commercial promotion. Information may be shared only in the limited circumstances listed below:

  • Advertising partners. We may share your advertising identifier with official ad partners to display ads and measure their performance. These partners are bound by confidentiality and may use the data only for the services they provide.
  • Analytics and service providers. We may provide aggregated, non identifying statistics to analytics providers so they can help us improve the product. This data cannot be traced back to any individual.
  • Authorized vendors. Service providers such as cloud hosting operators and crash analysis tools may access the limited data they need to do their work, and they must follow our privacy standards.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information when required by law, a court order, or an official regulatory request. In such cases we provide only the minimum data needed to meet the legal obligation.

4. How We Protect Your Data

We apply a combination of technical and organizational measures to guard your data against unauthorized access, alteration, leakage and loss:

  • Data transferred between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS technology. Stored personal data is protected with strong encryption.
  • Access to sensitive information is restricted to designated staff who need it for their work, and all such staff are required to keep it confidential.
  • We carry out regular security reviews, vulnerability checks and system updates to respond to emerging risks.

Please note that no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We work hard to protect your information, but we cannot guarantee absolute security during network transmission.

5. How Long We Keep Information

We store information only for as long as it is needed, and we apply different retention rules depending on the type of data:

  • Your account information, route progress and profile content are kept while your account is active. After you request deletion, this content is removed promptly.
  • Gameplay records, system logs and technical data are stored for up to 9 months, after which they are anonymized or deleted.
  • Data that must be retained to meet legal obligations or to prevent fraud is kept until those obligations are fulfilled.

6. How to Delete Your Account and Data

You may ask us to permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do this:

  1. Inside the Game. Open ArrowRove - Route Quest, go to Settings and then Account and Privacy, choose the delete account option, and follow the instructions on screen.
  2. By email. Send a message to our support mailbox with the subject ArrowRove Account and Data Deletion Request. Please include the email address tied to your account so we can verify your identity.

We will review your request and complete the deletion within 14 business days, then send you a confirmation. Please remember that once your data is removed, your progress, rewards and account details cannot be recovered.

7. Third Party Services

The Game includes tools provided by outside companies, such as advertising platforms, social login services and crash analysis tools. Each of these third parties operates under its own privacy policy. We do not control how they process data, and we recommend that you read their privacy terms before using the related functions.

8. Children and Privacy

ArrowRove - Route Quest is designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have received personal data from a minor without proper consent, we will delete it from our servers as soon as possible. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has shared personal information with us, please contact us so we can arrange for its removal.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our service or in applicable laws. For significant updates that affect how we collect or share information, we will notify you at least 7 days in advance through an in app notice or an email to your account. The latest version will always be available on the privacy page of the Game. Your continued use of the Service after the policy takes effect means you accept the updated terms.
